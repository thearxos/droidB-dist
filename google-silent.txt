# ARXOS droidB: Google components installed silently through Play system updates,
# with no prompt and no consent. `droidB debloat silent` removes them WITH their data;
# `droidB debloat check` reports any that a later update put back.
#
# Android System SafetyCore: an on-device image classifier that other Google apps (Google
# Messages' Sensitive Content Warnings) send pictures to. It holds no storage permission of
# its own; it does hold INTERNET. Installed by the Play Store without asking.
com.google.android.safetycore
# Android System Key Verifier: contact key verification. Installed by the Play Store without
# asking, seconds after SafetyCore. Holds QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES (sees every installed app) and INTERNET.
com.google.android.contactkeys
